## Cost Analysis & Economics
- **Context Window:** Up to 128K tokens across 72B and Max tiers.
- **Scaling Value:** Qwen2.5-72B offers a high performance-to-cost ratio for enterprise self-hosting or API consumption, outperforming many mid-tier proprietary models at a fraction of the cost.
