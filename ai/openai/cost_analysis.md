# OpenAI Cost Analysis & Token Pricing

## Overview
OpenAI provides industry-standard frontier models (GPT-4o, reasoning models o1/o3-mini, and cost-efficient GPT-4o-mini).

## Cost Analysis & Economics
- **Reasoning Economics:** While `o1` is premium-priced for complex multi-step logic, `o3-mini` provides rapid, highly optimized reasoning at ~7x lower cost.
- **High-Volume Production:** `GPT-4o-mini` remains one of the most cost-effective micro-models for high-throughput classification and extraction tasks.
