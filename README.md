# BLUKIT: MAKE PEOPLE VIBE

**SPREAD VIBES. MAKE PEOPLE VIBE.**

Blukit is an app for every crowd. Whether you're in a movie hall, a theater, a stadium, a mall, at college, or even just at home with friends—Blukit lets you connect with people nearby without needing the internet.

---

## 🫂 PEOPLE (Public Vibes)
When you open Blukit, you're in the **PEOPLE** field. This is where you see everyone around you.
- **Spread Vibes**: Send a vibe to everyone and see the field light up.
- **Everyone Nearby**: See who else is in the stadium or theater with you.

## ✨ KNOWN (Private Vibes)
Want to sync more privately? Connect with someone from the field.
- **Mutual Connection**: Once you both agree to connect, you can send private vibes back and forth.
- **Known Only**: Switch to the **KNOWN** view to focus only on the people you've successfully tied with.

---

## 📱 THE BLUKIT HUB
Everything you need is integrated into the Hub:
- **Visual Picker**: Easily jump between **BLUKIT** (all people) and **KNOWN** (your ties). It shows exactly how many people are active in each.
- **Energy Bar**: Quickly check if your Bluetooth, WiFi, and GPS are ready to go. Toggle **Dark Mode** or **Low Battery Mode** right there.
- **Identity**: Change your nickname whenever you want. You start as "UNKNOWN".
- **Resets**: Clean up your vibe history or reset your profile in one tap from the radio bar.

---

## 🛠️ SIMPLE RULES
1. **Nearby Only**: You only see people who are actually close to you.
2. **Both Agree**: Private ties require both people to say "Yes."
3. **Everything Vanishes**: Vibes don't last forever. They clear out every 12 hours.
4. **No Internet Needed**: Blukit works entirely through your phone's radios.
