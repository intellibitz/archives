# blukit: spread vibes

**SPREAD VIBES. CONNECT ANYWHERE.**

blukit is for any crowd. Sports venues, movie halls, universities, subways—anywhere a crowd forms, blukit lets you connect and spread vibes without the internet. It is anonymous, safe, secure, and private.

---

## 👥 ALL (Public Vibes)
The **ALL** field is where you see everyone around you.
- **Anonymous Presence**: People appear as pulsing glimmers.
- **Blossom to Focus**: Tap any glimmer to blossom it into a full node, showing their identity. This signifies your focused attention.
- **Active vs. Idle**: Non-active users stay in the **IDLE** row, while those who speak move to the **ACTIVE** row in the hub.

## 🎯 FOCUS (Filtered Field)
Cut out the crowd and listen only to those you've blossomed.
- **Zero-Noise**: Only see and hear vibes from your focused students or friends.
- **Smart Logic**: Focus lights up the moment you blossom someone, letting you jump to your filtered field anytime.

## ✨ VIBES (Mutual Resonance)
Establish secure, persistent private group resonances.
- **Mutual Permission**: Use the Student Options Menu to request a **VIBE**. Once agreed, you are securely linked in a private group.
- **Secure Communication**: All group vibes are encrypted and private.

## 🤝 1-1 (Personal Vibes)
Intimate, ephemeral one-on-one whispers.
- **Direct resonance**: Start a **1-1** whisper contextual within an established vibe.
- **Instant Focus**: Click any message in the ticker to immediately Focus or Vibe with that student.

---

## 📱 THE HUB
Everything you need is integrated into the full-width Hub:
- **Presence Zone**: Unified cloud categorized by **ACTIVE** and **IDLE** rows.
- **Interaction Bar**: "TYPE TO SPREAD VIBES"—a full-width segmented bar with your centered node persona and integrated logo.
- **Privacy First**: blukit never collects data of any kind. No tracking. No telemetry. No location sharing.

---

## 🛠️ SIMPLE RULES
1. **Nearby Only**: Only see people who are actually close to you through phone-to-phone radios.
2. **Mutual Consent**: Private vibes require both people to agree.
3. **Everything Vanishes**: Vibes clear out every 12 hours.
4. **Offline First**: Works entirely through your phone's radios.
