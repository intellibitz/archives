# Privacy Policy for Blukit

**Effective Date: August 10, 2026**

Blukit ("we", "us", or "our") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains our practices regarding the collection, use, and disclosure of information through our mobile application, Blukit.

## 1. Zero Data Collection
Blukit is a decentralized, peer-to-peer messaging application. **We do not collect, store, or transmit any of your personal data to any external servers.**

*   **User Identity**: Your nickname and emoji avatar are stored strictly on your local device and are only shared with nearby peers via local radio links (Bluetooth/Wi-Fi).
*   **Messages**: Your chat history is stored in a local encrypted database on your device and is never uploaded to a cloud.
*   **Location Data**: While the app requires location permissions to perform Bluetooth scanning (on certain Android versions), **we never access, store, or share your actual GPS location.**

## 2. Peer-to-Peer Communication
All communications occur directly between devices within radio range (typically 50–100 meters). Messages are transmitted over local radio frequencies and do not pass through the internet or any third-party infrastructure.

## 3. Encryption
To ensure the privacy of your 1-on-1 conversations, Blukit uses:
*   **AES-256-GCM** encryption for all data in transit.
*   **Diffie-Hellman (ECDH)** key exchange to establish secure sessions between peers.
*   Keys are stored securely in the **Android Keystore System**.

## 4. Data Retention (Ephemeral Lifecycle)
Blukit is designed to be ephemeral.
*   **Automatic Purge**: All local chat logs are automatically deleted from your device after 12 hours.
*   **Manual Deletion**: You can clear your local history or delete your profile at any time within the app settings.

## 5. Third-Party Services
Blukit does not use any third-party analytics, advertising SDKs, or cloud services.

## 6. Changes to This Policy
We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected by updating the "Effective Date" at the top of this document.
