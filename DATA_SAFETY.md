## 2. Absolute Proximity Privacy
- **Absolute Proximity Privacy**: By design, **all Spheres are public and discoverable**, while **all Private Spheres are encrypted**. Your data never leaves the physical radio range of your device.
- **Privacy-Preserving Synthesis**: Blukit performs **Local Synthesis** of Sphere energy. Only aggregated insights (e.g., "High Resonance in the Kitchen") are shared as **Sphere Synthesis** metadata. Raw Echo content remains protected by hardware encryption.
- **Resonance Consensus**: Consensus on the **Resonance Canvas** is achieved via anonymous votes. No voter identity is linked to the priority Echo.
- **Sphere Boundaries**: If someone isn't in your immediate **Resonance Field**, they cannot sense your **Shouts**.
- **Anchored Sensing**: Private Spheres must be anchored to a parent Sphere to be surfaced in the field hierarchy, ensuring secure interaction layers remain discoverable by trusted Sources.
- **Branded Resonance**: **Whispers** and **Private Spheres** resonate in **Stealth Rose** and stay encrypted between you and your trusted circles, nested safely inside a public Sphere.
- **Resonance Isolation**: High-speed media transfers happen over direct encrypted WiFi ties, isolated from the public sensing radios.

## 3. Self-Cleaning Persistence
- **Echo Decay (Pruning)**: To keep your device light over a long journey, Blukit automatically deletes large media older than 90 days.
- **Pinned Preservation**: Any Echo you "Pin" to a **Resonance Canvas** is exempt from decay, ensuring your core records and memories are never lost.
- **Ledger-Sync**: Local storage uses a deterministic history log (LWW-CRDT) to ensure consistency across Spheres without central coordination.
- **Intelligent Stream Management**: The stream automatically clears non-pinned ephemeral Echoes after they sink into **The Ledger**, preventing local storage bloat.

## 4. Hardware Encryption
**Everything shared in a Private Sphere is end-to-end encrypted.**

- **AES-256-GCM**: Whispers are scrambled so only the intended Sphere members can resonate with them.
- **ECDH Handshakes**: Secure keys are negotiated locally over the air.
- **Participation & Ownership**: Public Spheres follow a creator-as-owner model. While anyone can sense a Sphere, participation is restricted to joined Sources. The default **"GLOBAL SPHERE"** has no owner and belongs to the field, with all Sources pre-joined.
